## Work

DeepSource, where I spent the last six years of my career, was [acquired by Harness](https://deepsource.com/blog/deepsource-joins-harness). Having spent my entire career at startups and early-stage companies, I now find myself at a company whose employees wouldn't fit on a single commercial airliner.

This has given me an opportunity to see how a larger engineering organization works, how decisions get made, and what changes as companies grow.  I suspect I'll have more to say about that soon.

## Software Follies

Over the last couple of months, I've been building little tools for myself, replacing subscriptions with software tailored to how I work. With how capable AI coding agents have become, this is easier than ever for someone who's spent years writing software.

But one question kept nagging at me: what about people who don't know how to code?

That question led me to an interesting essay on [malleable software](https://www.inkandswitch.com/essay/malleable-software/) by Ink & Switch.  I've been going down that rabbit hole ever since. Hopefully, something interesting will come of it.

## Reading and writing

I'm trying to read more, and more widely. Fiction, travel writing, essays, and the occasional technical paper.
I'm also trying to write more regularly, rather than waiting until I have something particularly important to say.

## Elsewhere

I've been thinking about spending more time away from a screen, and making room for interests that have very little to do with software.  After a decade in startups I have finally come to terms with the fact there is more to life than software.
